`earthLand.json` contains longitude/latitude coastline rings from Natural Earth's
1:110m land dataset, rounded to two decimal places. The globe draws these vectors
as a monochrome map at runtime.

[Source data](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson)
