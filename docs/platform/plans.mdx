Your plan belongs to your account and covers every workspace you own. See it,
change it, and add a payment method in the dashboard under Settings > Billing.
Current prices and per-plan limits are on the
[pricing page](https://lazycloud.dev/pricing).

Billing shows your balance and monthly usage limit, which are worth a look
before a long job. A saved card unlocks your plan's GPU access but adds no
credit.

## What you pay for

Compute charges include a base rate per running container, plus CPU, memory,
and GPU rates. Startup, model loading, and warm idle time count toward runtime.
Volumes bill by the GiB-month stored. Compute, storage, and transfer charges
all deduct from one prepaid credit balance. Trial credit, subscription credit,
and purchased credit fund that same balance. Subscription fees are separate.

Direct downloads through signed object-storage URLs are free. LazyCloud meters
responses served through the API and workload internet egress.

The Usage page shows spend over time, broken down by app and workload. Add
credit in Settings > Billing, or save an automatic reload threshold and amount.
Purchased credit does not expire. Trial and subscription credit expire on their
stated dates.

Stopping pods you no longer need and shorter
[`keep_warm`](/concepts/scaling#warm-containers) windows on functions and
endpoints reduce idle charges. Downloading an output does not delete the
stored copy, so unused files need removing separately.

## Automatic reload

Automatic reload charges your saved card when your balance reaches the threshold
you choose. Trial, subscription, and purchased credit all count toward that
balance. Select a suggested amount or choose Custom amount to enter your own.
The billing form shows the permitted credit purchase range.

Buying credit, enabling automatic reload, and changing plans each need a
payment method in Billing. Update payment method replaces a saved card.
Invoices and the monthly usage limit remain available without a saved card.

A declined payment or one that needs bank authentication pauses automatic
reload. Reload resumes in Billing after you update the payment method, and
checkout still adds credit meanwhile. Payments already processing may finish
after you disable reload.

The monthly usage limit applies to metered usage, not card payments. A reload
can add credit beyond that limit. Unused purchased credit stays in your
balance.

## Compute pricing

The pricing page lists base rates for automatic placement. LazyCloud chooses
where the workload runs, and workloads allow interruptions. These rates have
no regional surcharge. LazyCloud meters compute per second, even when the
pricing page shows hourly rates.

One CPU is one vCPU and matches `cpu=1` in a workload definition. CPU and memory
each bill at the greater of the reserved amount and measured usage.

GPU rates are per card. Add the CPU and memory charges for the same workload
size to get the total compute cost. GPU availability depends on the model and
whether the workload allows interruptions.

Pinning a region or availability zone requires a plan that allows it. Placement
choices apply the following multipliers to base compute prices:

| Workload setting | CPU and memory | GPU |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Non-preemptible execution | 3× | 1× |
| Pinned region or availability zone | 1.5× | 1.5× |
| Both | 4.5× | 1.5× |

Storage and transfer rates do not change with these choices. Volume storage
includes artifacts. The quoted rate uses a 30-day month, and charges prorate to
the time stored.

For a connected cloud account, the management fee is a percentage of base
compute rates. Your cloud provider bills the underlying machines separately.
Storage and transfer keep their full published rates.

The [pricing catalog](https://lazycloud.dev/api/v1/pricing) lists published
rates, regional multipliers, and the date the rates take effect.

## Plans

Free has no subscription fee or minimum term. New accounts receive a one-time
trial credit, with no recurring Free allowance.

Every plan can use all offered GPU models with a saved payment method and a
positive credit balance, subject to GPU availability.

Team includes monthly usage credit, higher concurrency, custom domains, and
three members total, including the owner. The member limit spans all
workspaces the account owns, and each person counts once.

Business adds unlimited members, more usage credit, higher concurrency, and
longer log and artifact retention. It also lets you connect one AWS account
and create workspaces that run inside it.
Joining your own machines is available on every plan.

Plan changes happen in Settings > Billing, under Change plan. You cannot move
to a plan whose limits your account already exceeds. The dashboard names what
has to go first. While a plan change is processing, you cannot add
plan-limited resources.

## Without a payment method

An account with no card on file has lower concurrency limits and can use only
the trial GPU models listed on the pricing page. Adding a card unlocks the
plan's full concurrency and GPU selection, but adds no credit. Work still
requires a positive credit balance.

## When you reach a limit

Starting a container past your concurrency limit fails with a message naming
the limit. LazyCloud checks workspace and member limits when you create those
resources. Reaching a concurrency limit does not stop existing work.

When your credit balance reaches zero, LazyCloud blocks new work and transfers,
and the usage monitor stops running compute. Transfers already under way may
finish. LazyCloud records usage periodically, so the balance can go negative
before work stops. Added credit covers that negative balance first.

You can also save a monthly usage limit across your workspaces. It counts usage
paid with any credit and resets on the first of each month at midnight UTC.
Reaching it stops compute and blocks new work and transfers in the same way.
Subscription fees do not count toward this limit. No limit removes it, and $0
stops new work and transfers. Running compute can exceed the limit between
usage records and shutdown.

If funds run out, LazyCloud keeps existing stored data for 30 days, then
deletes it. Credit that restores a positive balance within that period keeps
the data. Storage is free during this grace period.
