title: "Plans and limits"
description: "What your plan includes, what usage costs, and what happens when you reach a limit."
---

Your plan belongs to your account and covers every workspace you own. See it,
change it, and add a payment method in the dashboard under Settings. Current
prices and per-plan limits are on the
[pricing page](https://lazycloud.dev/pricing).

## What you pay for

Compute is metered per second for each running container: a base rate per
container, plus rates per CPU, per GiB of memory, and per GPU. Volumes
are billed by the GiB-month stored. Compute, storage, and transfer charges all
deduct from one prepaid credit balance. Trial credit, subscription credit, and
purchased credit fund that same balance. Subscription fees are separate.

Direct downloads through signed object-storage URLs are not charged. Responses
served through the API and workload internet egress are metered.

The Usage page shows spend over time, broken down by app and workload. Add
credit in Settings, or save an automatic reload threshold and amount. Purchased
credit does not expire. Trial and subscription credit expire on their stated
dates.

## Compute pricing

The pricing page lists base rates for automatic placement. LazyCloud chooses
where the workload runs, and workloads allow interruptions. These rates have
no regional surcharge. The hour/second toggle changes the displayed unit;
compute is always metered per second. Hourly GPU prices show two decimals;
CPU and memory prices show up to four.
Per-second prices show up to six decimals for GPUs and eight for CPU and memory.
Billing uses the full rate before display rounding.

One CPU is one vCPU and matches `cpu=1` in a workload definition. CPU and memory
are each billed at the greater of the reserved amount and measured usage.

GPU rates are per card. Add the CPU and memory charges for the same workload
size to calculate total compute costs. GPU availability depends on the model
and whether the workload allows interruptions.

Pinning a region or availability zone requires a plan that allows it. Placement
choices apply the following multipliers to base compute prices:

| Workload setting | CPU and memory | GPU |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Non-preemptible execution | 3× | 1× |
| Pinned region or availability zone | 1.5× | 1.5× |
| Both | 4.5× | 1.5× |

Storage and transfer rates do not change with these choices. Volume storage
includes artifacts and uses a 30-day month for the quoted rate, with charges
prorated to the time stored.

For a connected cloud account, the management fee is a percentage of base compute
rates. Your cloud provider bills the underlying machines separately. Storage and
transfer retain their full published rates.

The [pricing catalog](https://lazycloud.dev/api/v1/pricing) lists published
rates, regional multipliers, and the date the rates take effect.

## Plans

Free has no subscription fee or minimum term. New accounts receive a one-time
trial credit, with no recurring Free allowance.

Every plan can use all offered GPU models with a saved payment method and a
positive credit balance, subject to GPU availability.

Team is a monthly subscription with included usage credit, higher
limits, unlimited members, and two features Free does not have: connecting
your own AWS account and serving deployments from custom domains. Joining your
own machines is available on every plan. Business includes more usage credit,
higher concurrency limits, and longer log retention.

Change plans from Settings, then Manage subscription. You cannot move to a
plan whose limits your account already exceeds; the dashboard names what has
to go first. While a plan change is processing, you cannot add plan-limited
resources.

## Without a payment method

An account with no card on file has lower concurrency limits and can use only
the trial GPU models listed on the pricing page. Adding a card allows the
plan's full concurrency and GPU selection; it does not add credit. Work still
requires a positive credit balance.

## When you reach a limit

Starting a container past your concurrency limit fails with a message naming
the limit. Workspace and member limits are checked when those resources are
created. Existing work is not stopped for a concurrency limit.

When your credit balance reaches zero, new work and transfers are blocked and
the usage monitor stops running compute. Already-started transfers may finish.
Usage is recorded periodically, so the balance can become negative before work
stops. Added credit covers that negative balance first.

You can also save a monthly usage limit across your workspaces. It counts usage
paid with any credit and resets on the first of each month at midnight UTC.
Reaching it stops compute and blocks new work and transfers in the same way.
Subscription fees do not count toward this limit.

If funds run out, existing stored data is retained for 30 days before deletion.
Add enough credit to restore a positive balance during that period to retain
your data. Storage is not charged during this grace period.
