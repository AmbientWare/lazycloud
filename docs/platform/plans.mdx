---
title: "Plans and limits"
description: "What your plan includes, what usage costs, and what happens when you reach a limit."
---

Your plan belongs to your account and covers every workspace you own. See it,
change it, and add a payment method in the dashboard under Settings. Current
prices and per-plan limits are on the
[pricing page](https://lazycloud.dev/pricing).

## What you pay for

Compute is metered per second for each running container: a base rate per
container, plus rates per CPU core, per GiB of memory, and per GPU. Volumes
are billed by the GiB-month stored. Each plan includes an amount of usage per
billing period; usage beyond that is invoiced monthly and charged to the card
on file. The Usage page in the dashboard shows spend over time, broken down by
app and workload.

## Plans

Free has no subscription and no minimum term. It includes a small monthly
usage allowance and caps how many apps, members, and simultaneously running
containers your account can have.

Team is a monthly subscription with a larger included allowance, higher
limits, unlimited members, and two features Free does not have: connecting
your own AWS account and serving deployments from custom domains. Joining your
own machines is available on both plans.

Change plans from Settings, then Manage subscription. You cannot move to a
plan whose limits your account already exceeds; the dashboard names what has
to go first. While a plan change is processing, you cannot add plan-limited
resources.

## Without a payment method

An account with no card on file gets a small one-time spending allowance and a
low cap on running containers, whatever plan it is on. When that allowance is
spent, new work is refused and running containers are stopped. Adding a card
lifts both immediately and moves the account onto its plan's terms.

## When you reach a limit

Limits are checked when something new starts, never against work that is
already running.

Starting a container past your concurrency limit fails with a message naming
the limit. Creating an app past the app limit, or adding a member past the
member limit, fails the same way. Nothing that is running is affected.

If a payment fails, the account is past due. New work is refused until you
update the card; running work is left alone.
