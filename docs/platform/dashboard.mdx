The dashboard at [lazycloud.dev](https://lazycloud.dev) is where you sign in,
approve CLI logins, watch your workloads, and manage your account.

## Workspace pages

Apps lists every app in the workspace with its workloads, recent activity, and
version history. From an app you can pause, resume, scale, and delete
workloads, inspect pod instances, watch latency, and call a workload from the
browser with the request playground.

Tasks is the workspace's task history. Each task shows its status, timing,
retries, result, and logs.

Storage holds volumes, secrets, and the queue and map collections, with a
browser for each and links back to the workloads that use them.

Sandboxes are listed under their app, with an in-browser terminal, a file
browser, and a process list for each running one.

Usage shows spend over time, broken down by app and workload. It answers for
the account, so the figure is the same whichever workspace you are in.

## Settings

Settings opens over the current page and is about your account rather than a
workspace.

General shows your GitHub identity and your plan: what is included, what you
have used, how many containers are running against your limit, and the buttons
to manage the subscription and payment method.

Tokens creates and deletes access tokens for the CLI, CI, and direct API calls.

Compute is where you connect an AWS account, adjust what it may launch, and
join your own machines. See [Compute](/platform/compute).

Domains registers hostnames you own. See [Custom domains](/platform/domains).

Workspace creation, renaming, and members are in the workspace menu at the top
of the page.
